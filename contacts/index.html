FAQ

Common Questions

How long does an app take to build?

An MVP typically takes 8–14 weeks. A full-featured product is 4–6 months. We'll give you an accurate estimate after the discovery phase.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Absolutely. We love working with founders to validate ideas fast. Our MVP track is designed specifically for pre-seed and seed-stage teams.

Can you take over an existing app?

Yes. We conduct a technical audit first, then provide a clear roadmap for improvements, refactoring or feature additions.

What's included in post-launch support?

All projects include 30 days of bug-fix support. Ongoing SLA retainers covering updates, monitoring and new features are available.
